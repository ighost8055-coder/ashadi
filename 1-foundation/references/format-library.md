# Kapruka Ad Format Library — derived from the Style-Board

> **What this is:** the menu of **static ad formats / devices** distilled from the Style-Board (`style-board/_style-board.md`, 48 hand-picked examples). Two jobs:
> 1. **Format menu** — the Art Director / Ideator pick a format in plain words for each idea (the "Image" column).
> 2. **Idea source (NEW, 2026-07-05)** — the Style-Board is now an **idea-derivation source**, not taste-only. Take a *format* here + a Kapruka *persona/angle* + the *moat* → a fresh ad concept. Ideas derived this way carry Source **`Style-Board`**.
>
> **Still honest, still on-brand:** every derived concept obeys the moat + Three-Yes Test + brand colours (purple-dominant, one yellow accent, real premium photography — see `taste-digest.md`). Emulate the *technique/energy*, never another brand's product, logo, or copy.

---

## HOW TO DERIVE AN IDEA FROM A FORMAT (the method)

`[Style-Board format/device]  +  [persona or general]  +  [the moat / a real truth]  =  an ad concept`

> **Worked example:** *phone-as-portal* device (SB-033) + diaspora persona + the delivery moat → **"a phone held in one hand, a birthday cake spilling out of the screen into a hand in Sri Lanka"** — dramatises *"order from Sydney → it arrives on her table."* Hook: *"Send it from your phone. She holds it tomorrow."*

Pick the format that fits the message: a fear-killer wants **bold type on flat colour**; a delivery-truth wants **phone-as-portal** or **doorway-as-frame**; a gifting-emotion wants **candid handheld** or **many-hands abundance**; an Ecom "everything store" wants a **grid**.

## THE FORMATS (grouped)

### A. Native-UI mimicry — *reads as content, not an ad*
| Format | What it is | Best for (Kapruka) | SB |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CAPTCHA-as-ad** | "Select all squares with [gifts]… Verify" rebuilt as the poster on one flat colour | Ecom selection · playful gift-picking | SB-021 |
| **iOS alert / notification** | A native phone alert dialog laid over a candid photo | Reminders, urgency ("Delivery scheduled ✓") | SB-025 |
| **Calendar-event overlay** | A native iOS calendar block over a candid — frames it as a scheduled ritual | Occasion lead-time ("Mother's Day — order by the 8th") | SB-040 |
| **Faux-Instagram post card** | A real product photo nested in a clean IG post card | Social proof, "received-it" moments | SB-030 |
| **Phone-as-portal** ⭐ | Product spilling out of a phone screen into a real hand | **The delivery moat** — order on phone → arrives in real life | SB-033 |
| **Photoshop layers panel** | Product rebuilt as a named-layer stack | "What's in the box / hamper," Ecom | SB-035 |
| **Quiz / "pick one" + input field** | Options with badges + a native answer field | Engagement, gift-finder | SB-028 |

### B. Conceptual device / visual metaphor — *built from the product's own world*
| Format | What it is | Best for | SB |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product-as-object metaphor** | The product arranged *as* something else (fries→handcuffs) | Witty single-idea heroes | SB-026 |
| **Landmark / place-as-device** | Product composited into a recognisable landmark | Diaspora ("from your city → home") | SB-004, SB-047 |
| **Element-as-frame** | A real element (road curve, doorway arch) becomes the layout frame | Cultural moments, journeys | SB-022, SB-041 |
| **Magnifier "closer look"** | A held magnifying glass zooms the one proof point | Trust — "see for yourself" (freshness, 23 yrs) | SB-031 |
| **Torn-paper hand-burst** | A hand pushes the product up through a ripped flat ground | Surprise, reveal, energy | SB-032 |
| **Quadrant / axis map** | Product cut-outs on a 2-axis "find your match" map | Ecom range, gift-matching | SB-027 |
