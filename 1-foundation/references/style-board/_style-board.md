# Kapruka Style Board — preferred-style examples (TASTE ONLY)

> **What this is:** a mood board of pins the human hand-picked as **examples of the look/vibe they like** — fun, exciting, engaging, vibrant, social-media-worthy, aesthetic (iPhone/editorial/conceptual). It teaches the **aesthetic** of Kapruka's ads.
>
> **What this is — TWO jobs (updated 2026-07-05):**
> 1. **Taste** — the aesthetic/energy to match when building creatives (distilled in `../taste-digest.md`).
> 2. **Idea source** — the **formats/devices** here are now an **idea-derivation source**. Take a format + a Kapruka persona/angle + the moat → an ad concept (Source `Style-Board`). The distilled format menu + derivation method live in `../format-library.md`.
>
> **Still NOT:**
> - ❌ NOT to be copied 1:1. Emulate the *technique/energy* only; never the original brand's product, logo, or copy.
> - ❌ NOT on-ad reference links — derive the format/idea; the designer sources their own references.
>
> **Who reads it:** the **Ideator** (derive concepts from formats, via `../format-library.md`) and the **Art Director / Designer / CD** (match the vibe).
