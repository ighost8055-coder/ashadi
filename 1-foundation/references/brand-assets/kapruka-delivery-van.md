## Exact description
A **compact Japanese-style micro-van** (kei-van, Suzuki Every / Every-Wagon type — small, tall, cab-over), shown in three views (front 3/4, side profile, rear 3/4).
- **Body colour:** Kapruka Purple **`#4B2386`** (per the official spec sheet — see palette note below), full wrap.
- **Signature graphic:** a large **Kapruka-Yellow `#FFD21E` smile swoosh** sweeping up across the lower front wing and side/rear panels.
- **Wordmark:** white **"kapruka"** logo (the **'u' formed by a small yellow smile**) with the tagline **"A world of choices"** beneath — on the front doors, and again on the rear side panel.
- **Icon pattern:** a grid of white line-icons (gift, cake, flower, electronics, etc. — "A world of choices") printed across the **rear side windows/panel**.
- **Wheels:** plain black steel rims. **Plate:** Sri Lankan, "WP PG 2690".
- **Spec (from sheet):** *Compact & Agile* — 3395 mm (L) × 1475 mm (W) × 1890 mm (H).

## ✅ Palette note — RESOLVED (2026-07-18)
"New for assets only": the **real van keeps `#4B2386` / `#FFD21E`** (attach it as-is, don't recolour). The **poster ground + type stay `#340A5C` / `#FFC107`** until a formal rebrand. Tagline **"A world of choices"** (already on the van) is the brand default. See `_brand-assets.md`.

## The usage rule (global)
1. **Attach `kapruka-delivery-van.png`** whenever a concept needs a delivery vehicle: *"Use the attached Kapruka delivery van image exactly as provided — composite/match it; do not redraw, recolour or restyle the livery, wordmark, smile or icon pattern from text."*
2. **Never a generic/unbranded van** stands in for delivery. The real branded van (attached) is the only sanctioned vehicle, used when the concept genuinely calls for one.
3. After generating, **check the livery, wordmark, smile and plate** — rebuild as vectors for final production (brand guidelines §11).
