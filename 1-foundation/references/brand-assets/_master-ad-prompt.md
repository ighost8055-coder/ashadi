# MASTER PROMPT — PREMIUM COMMERCIAL ADVERTISING CREATIVE

> **Status (human-supplied, 2026-10-12):** the premium-quality baseline for every image prompt, read **BEFORE the final image-generation prompt is written**. Used by the Reference Recreator (`2-roles/12-reference-recreator.md`) and the Telegram/ig2img pipeline (`tools/ig2img/lib/prompt.mjs`, all three modes: recreate · idea · promote).
>
> **Precedence:** this is a *quality bar*, not a replacement rulebook. Where it conflicts with `_image-gen-rules.md` or the root `CLAUDE.md` Core Rules, **those win** — specifically: Inter only (not "contemporary" typography effects), Light-on-Text (Rule 16: headline + at most one support line), the Two-Pass type rule (§A0b — no drop shadows/outlines/scene light baked into type), no subtext (Rule 19), vibrant-not-oversaturated (Rule 14), real kapruka.com products only, and the Living Frame Test. The placeholders below (`[BRAND NAME]` …) are filled from the brief; Kapruka's values are fixed: Brand = Kapruka, Colors = `#340A5C` / `#FFC107` / `#D9A441` / `#F6C96A` / `#F5F4EB`, Format = 4:5 (3:4 on the Telegram pipeline), Logo = the attached asset only.

---

Act as an elite advertising agency's Creative Director, Senior Art Director, Commercial Photographer, CGI Artist, and High-End Retouching Specialist.

Create a visually stunning, premium, professional advertising creative for the following brand and campaign.

**CAMPAIGN DETAILS**

- Brand: [BRAND NAME]
- Product or Service: [PRODUCT/SERVICE]
- Main Campaign Message: [MAIN MESSAGE]
- Target Audience: [TARGET AUDIENCE]
- Advertisement Format: [INSTAGRAM POST 4:5 / STORY 9:16 / SQUARE 1:1]
- Creative Direction: [LIFESTYLE PHOTOGRAPHY / PRODUCT HERO AD / PREMIUM CGI / DIGITAL SERVICE AD]
- Brand Colors: [BRAND COLOR PALETTE]
- Headline: [EXACT HEADLINE]
- Supporting Text: [EXACT SUPPORTING TEXT]
- CTA: [CALL TO ACTION]

## 1. ABSOLUTE VISUAL QUALITY

The finished creative must look like an authentic, professionally commissioned advertisement produced by a leading international creative agency.

Achieve exceptional perceived sharpness, clean edges, refined micro-details, rich tonal depth, controlled highlights, beautiful contrast, realistic materials, and meticulous digital finishing.

The final result must feel premium, expensive, captivating, highly polished, and ready for publication in a major national advertising campaign.

No generic AI-generated aesthetic. No unfinished or amateur-looking composition. Every element must appear intentional.

Aim for the quality of professional commercial photography combined with the creative precision of expert Photoshop compositing and premium advertising graphic design.

## 2. PHOTOGRAPHY AND REALISM

Where people, environments, or physical products are present, use high-end commercial photography aesthetics.

- Realistic natural skin texture and facial details
- Anatomically correct hands, fingers, and body proportions
- Beautifully controlled studio-quality or cinematic natural lighting
- Authentic reflections, shadows, and material textures
- Clear subject separation from the background
- Purposeful depth of field without excessive blur
- Natural expressions, believable interactions, and realistic poses
- Convincing product scale, proportions, and perspective
- Professionally balanced highlights and shadows

Every important subject must be clear, detailed, and visually compelling.

Avoid waxy skin, plastic-looking humans, distorted anatomy, artificial facial expressions, or unrealistic objects.

## 3. COMMERCIAL LIGHTING AND COLOR GRADING

Use sophisticated, advertising-grade color treatment.

Create rich, visually engaging colors that enhance the brand identity while maintaining believable textures and natural skin tones.

Use luminous highlights, refined contrast, controlled saturation, accurate white balance, and realistic light interaction.

Brand colors should be bold, memorable, and consistent.

When appropriate, use subtle rim lighting, soft studio fill, attractive specular highlights, and realistic atmospheric depth.

Colors must feel vibrant and premium without becoming oversaturated, neon-heavy, or visually harsh.

Avoid muddy shadows, washed-out colors, flat exposure, and excessive HDR effects.

## 4. PROFESSIONAL ART DIRECTION AND COMPOSITION

Construct the image using the standards of high-end commercial advertising and professional visual communication.

Establish an unmistakable visual hierarchy:

1. Immediate attention-grabbing focal point
2. Strong, unmistakable product or service recognition
3. Clear and impactful main headline
4. Supporting information and branding
5. Visible call to action when appropriate

Apply a carefully controlled visual grid, balanced spacing, strategic negative space, thoughtful framing, and professional alignment.

Use foreground, middle ground, and background depth where appropriate.

Introduce sophisticated graphic elements, gradients, geometric shapes, shadows, and highlights only when they improve the communication.

Maintain a strong visual focal point and avoid unnecessary decorative clutter.

The advertisement must be captivating at first glance and easily understandable on a mobile screen.

## 5. PREMIUM TYPOGRAPHY

Treat typography as a major advertising design element, not an afterthought.

Use bold, contemporary, professional typography suited to the campaign personality and target market.

Give the headline strong visual presence using carefully controlled scale, spacing, contrast, and placement.

Where stylistically appropriate, use professional layered text effects, tasteful outlines, refined drop shadows, dimensional styling, or contrasting colored lettering.

All typography must be visually coherent, readable, correctly aligned, and harmoniously integrated into the composition.

Render only the exact supplied text.

Do not invent additional promotional messages, prices, claims, slogans, or brand names.

For Sinhala language content, prioritize correct Sinhala characters, spelling, word spacing, letter shapes, and legibility. If accurate typography cannot be produced, leave a clean, intentional area for the exact text to be added manually.

## 6. PRODUCT PRESENTATION AND BRAND ACCURACY

The product or service must appear as the hero of the advertisement.

Create visually appealing, commercially persuasive product presentation with accurate proportions, realistic materials, appropriate reflections, and attractive lighting.

If original product photos, logos, packaging images, or app screenshots are provided, preserve their identity, proportions, visual markings, and brand colors.

Do not creatively reinterpret, distort, replace, or hallucinate important brand details.

Avoid inaccurate packaging, fake text, misspelled logos, or incorrect interface elements.

Ensure the product looks desirable, recognizable, and professionally marketed.

## 7. HIGH-END GRAPHIC DESIGN AND COMPOSITING

All visual elements must look professionally integrated, as if finished by an expert commercial retoucher and senior graphic designer.

Use carefully constructed layer relationships, precise object edges, realistic contact shadows, refined masking, consistent lighting direction, and cohesive color grading.

Make every element feel part of one unified advertisement.

Avoid obvious cutouts, inconsistent lighting, harsh artificial outlines, random floating objects, and unrealistic shadows.

Any graphical embellishments must strengthen the campaign, not distract from the product.

## 8. MAXIMUM CLARITY AND DETAIL

Prioritize:

- Razor-sharp product edges
- Clean and legible typographic shapes
- Highly defined packaging details
- Detailed and natural photographic textures
- Smooth, artifact-free gradients
- Controlled local contrast
- Visually clean color transitions
- High-quality finishing across the entire frame

Avoid pixelation, compression artifacts, fuzzy text, smeared details, oversharpening halos, noisy gradients, unintentional blur, and low-quality AI textures.

The image must remain impactful and professionally readable at normal Instagram mobile viewing size.

## 9. ORIGINAL CREATIVE CONCEPT

Do not simply place the product in the center of a background.

Develop a creative, memorable, brand-relevant advertising concept that communicates a clear benefit, emotion, or lifestyle connection.

Use a visual storytelling approach appropriate to the intended campaign.

The composition should feel thoughtfully art-directed, not randomly generated.

The creative should be unique to this brand and product rather than following a generic advertising template.

## 10. FINAL QUALITY CONTROL

Before finalizing, critically evaluate the image as an experienced advertising creative director.

Check the following:

- Does the advertisement look professionally art-directed?
- Is the main subject immediately recognizable?
- Are product and branding elements accurate?
- Are all people, objects, and materials believable?
- Are the colors attractive and consistent?
- Are image details crisp without artificial sharpening?
- Is the layout balanced and intentional?
- Is the typography accurate and readable?
- Is the creative compelling on a mobile screen?
- Would this look credible as a polished, paid social media campaign?

Correct visible flaws before presenting the final image.

**FINAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENT**

Produce one exceptional, publication-ready, premium commercial advertisement with outstanding clarity, captivating visual impact, precise composition, sophisticated brand styling, realistic detail, vibrant controlled colors, and meticulous high-end finishing.

The result should resemble professionally photographed, professionally art-directed, and expertly retouched advertising work — not a quick, generic AI-generated poster.

Do not include any phone screenshots, social media interface elements, watermarks, or irrelevant visual content in the actual creative.
