## Exact description (for models that can't take a reference image, or as a spelling/detail check)
A **deep Retail Purple `#340A5C`** rigid square cake box, photographed at a 3/4 angle on a pale surface. Features, top to bottom:
- **Lid:** a single large, bold **Kapruka-Yellow `#FFC107`** upward-curving **smile** (open-U shape), centred.
- Beneath the smile, a small line of **white uppercase** text: **"EVERY BITE. PURE HAPPINESS."** (the tagline).
- **Front lower band:** on the left, the small **Kapruka lion mascot** (a friendly yellow/green lion character); centred, the white **"kapruka"** wordmark with its small strapline; on the right, a small **QR code** in white.
- Clean matte finish, subtle real cardboard edges, soft natural studio light — premium, not glossy/plastic.

## The usage rule (global — applies to every image-gen prompt this system produces)
1. **Attach `kapruka-cake-box.png` to the generation tool** whenever the concept's gift is a cake box, and instruct: *"Use the attached Kapruka cake box image exactly as provided — composite/match it; do not redraw, distort, recolour or reconstruct the smile, tagline, wordmark, lion mascot or QR from text."*
2. **Never substitute a generic/unbranded wrapped box** for "a gift." (See Reference Recreator Hard Rule 11.)
3. The **cake inside/beside** is still swapped to a real kapruka.com listing. **⚠️ Vary which cake, run to run — don't default to a ribbon cake every time.** Real options span *Purple Passion* (vanilla-&-chocolate with purple icing flowers), chocolate gateaux, butter-icing-covered cakes, bento/mini cakes, fruit gateaux, cheesecake-style listings, and yes, ribbon cakes too — but only as one option among several, picked because it fits the concept, not by default.
4. Same as the logo: after generating, **check the smile shape, tagline spelling, mascot and QR** — AI models distort packaging; for final production rebuild as vectors (brand guidelines §11).
