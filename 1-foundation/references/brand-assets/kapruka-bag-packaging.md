## Exact description

- Clean matte finish, real paper texture, soft studio light — premium, not glossy.

## The usage rule (global)
1. **Attach `kapruka-bag-packaging.png`** and instruct: *"Use the attached Kapruka shopping bag exactly as provided — composite/match it; do not redraw, recolour or reconstruct the smile, wordmark, tagline, icon pattern or QR from text."*
