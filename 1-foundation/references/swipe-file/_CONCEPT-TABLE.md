> **Not one of them shows the product being used correctly by a happy person.** One wastes it. One hides it in a reflection. One shows the wrong product entirely. One builds the logo out of flight paths. **Every single one takes an indirect route to the point.**
>
> **⛔ The failure this names:** this project's frames keep taking the **direct route** — here is the gift, here is the teacher, here is the moment, here is a respectful line about it. **Direct is why they read as stock. The concept IS the detour.**

# THE NINE CONCEPT BUCKETS

## ⭐ K1 · THE PRODUCT FAILS, AND THAT IS THE AD
**The product is wasted, spilled, destroyed, rejected or unwanted — and the brand says that's fine.**

- **1 Coca-Cola** — the drink is airborne, lost, *"worth every drop."*
- **13 Nike** — *"THIS IS TRASH."* The shoe **is** garbage, and that's the specification.
- **25 Road Lodge** — a one-star hotel **owning its one star**.
- **28 Monopoly** — *"For learning to let go"* over a child screaming at losing.

> **Why it works:** advertising is assumed to flatter, so a brand that doesn't gets believed. **Only usable when the flaw is literally true (Core Rule 9).**
> **⛔ Kapruka form:** joke about **the customer's situation** — forgot the date, panic-bought, sent the same thing as someone else. **Never about the delivery.** That's the moat, and it would be a false claim, not a joke.

## ⭐ K2 · THE PRODUCT IS NEVER SHOWN DIRECTLY
**It appears only as a reflection, a shadow, a consequence, or an effect on someone's body.**

- **3 Domino's** — the delivery exists **only in his sunglasses**.
- **22 Spotify** — **goosebumps on skin.** No phone, no headphones, no face.
- **34 ESPN** — two traffic cones become goalposts. **Nothing was added to the scene; it was only noticed.**

> **Why it works:** the viewer assembles it themselves, and what you assemble you believe. **This is Core Rule 17 as a DEVICE, not as permission to omit a product.**
> **⛔ Kapruka form:** the face of the person receiving · the emptied box · the table with one more plate on it · the phone screen at 6am.

## ⭐ K3 · SHOW THE WRONG THING
**The frame shows the failure, the counterfeit or the absence — never the correct product.**

- **12 VW** — denim shorts that do not fit, to sell **genuine parts**.
- **17 Aircel** — a goat painted in tiger stripes: *"Tigers are irreplaceable."*
- **16 Ghee** — pasta in a hair straightener.

> **Why it works:** showing the right thing teaches nothing. **Showing the wrong thing makes the viewer supply the right one.**
> **⛔ Kapruka form:** the gift that is technically correct and emotionally absurd. **This is the mechanism behind the cane idea — and it survives with a different object.**

## ⭐ K4 · THE BRAND IS BUILT OUT OF THE WORLD
**The logo or brand shape is constructed from the thing being advertised, never placed on top of it.**

- **14 McDonald's** — flight paths **draw the arches**.
- **15 Spotify** — the record's grooves **are** the running lanes.
- **4 KFC** — the logo sits **inline, mid-sentence, as a word**.

> **⛔ Kapruka's hard limit:** one logo, composited from the attached asset, **never redrawn (Invariant 4).** So: build a **brand-shaped composition** — a route, an arc, a colour field, a shape made of real products — and place the real logo once. **Building the mark itself out of flowers is banned.**

## ⭐ K5 · THE SAME FEELING IN A DIFFERENT UNIFORM
**Take the emotional situation and move it to a completely different world, keeping the body language identical.**

- **18** a tiger in a sherwani · **7/26 talabat** — a tailor feeding a man mid-fitting · **37 Uber Eats** — a beekeeper lifting his veil to eat a burger.

> **⭐ This is the human's own "teacher comes in all avatars" idea** — and it is the best campaign-generator in the set: one insight, infinite castings.
> **⛔ The craft rule: the funnier the idea, the straighter the craft.** The absurdity lives in the concept; the execution stays dead serious. The moment the rendering gets jokey too, it collapses into a meme.

## K6 · THE SENTENCE WITH A HOLE IN IT
**A word is literally missing, and the picture fills it.**

- **33/36 nib** — *"___ happens"* with a man falling through a ceiling **in the blank**.
- **11 B&Q** — a saw **cuts the apostrophe out of "can't."**

> **Why it works:** the ad **cannot be read** without the picture, so redundancy is structurally impossible. **Clears Core Rule 15** — one literal missing word is not a pun.

## K7 · ONE VARIABLE, TWICE
**Same subject, same everything, one thing changed.**

- **31/32 Fabrikant** — *"Uncomfortable / Comfortable."* One half has the chair.
- **19 Singapore Kindness** — one room split down the middle; two consequences of one act.

> **Why it works:** nothing is *transformed*, so **nothing is over-claimed.** Honest by construction.
> **⛔ The craft is the whole thing:** identical scale, angle, light and pose. Any drift and the comparison stops being a comparison.

## K8 · ONE SKELETON, MANY FILLINGS
**A fixed sentence stem with one variable per frame.**

- **24/30 Spotify** — *"Because music doesn't judge." / "Because mixtapes still work."*
- **20/21 Midea** — *"WINDOW UP / MATCH ON."*

> **⭐ This is how a batch stops being five unrelated posters.** It also argues range **by repetition** rather than by cramming products into a grid.

## K9 · SAY LESS THAN YOU EARNED
**Under-claim something true; hand the credit away.**

- **38 VW** — *"We didn't invent anything."* over a father with a child on his shoulders seeing over a crowd.

> **⭐ The safe form of K1** — humble, warm, fully honest, and it hands the credit to the person the ad is about. *(This is the bucket "We Didn't. Teach You. Anything." sits in — the one frame the human picked out of five.)*

## ⛔ THE UNNAMED TENTH BUCKET — what this project has been producing instead

**K0 · THE REVERENT TABLEAU.** *The product or its absence, beautifully lit, nobody present, a respectful line beneath it.*

Five Teachers' Day batches ran on it: a dusty cane · a cupboard of mugs · an empty classroom at golden hour · a margin of old handwriting · a cut cake on purple.

> **⛔ K0 does not appear once in 38 references.** It is what a system produces when it optimises **against bans** instead of **toward an idea**. Naming it is how it stops being the default.
>
> **The tell:** if the frame could be captioned *"we honour teachers"* and still work, it is K0. **Rebuild it as K1–K9.**

## HOW TO USE THIS

1. **Recognition Brief** (role 15) → **WHAT** the ad is about: an evidenced, relatable scene.
2. **⭐ THIS FILE** → **WHICH DETOUR** the concept takes. Declare `Concept: K1`–`K9`.
3. **Execution Table** → **HOW** it is built: `V × C × T · light · copy shape`.
4. **§A0 Living Frame Test** → a person, an event, two people, someone coming off badly.

> **⛔ `Concept: K0` is not a valid declaration.** It is the name of the failure, kept here only so it can be recognised and rejected.
