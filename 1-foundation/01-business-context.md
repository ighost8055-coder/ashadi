## 1. What Kapruka Is

Kapruka is Sri Lanka's largest locally-owned e-commerce and gifting platform (kapruka.com). It operates **three** distinct businesses under one brand, serving different buyer mindsets: **Gifting**, **Corporate** (B2B / bulk / branded gifting), and **Ecom** (self-purchase).

**The moat (the one thing to anchor almost every ad to):**
Reliable, island-wide delivery — including from *anywhere in the world to anywhere in Sri Lanka* — on a date the buyer chooses, backed by 23+ years of operation. This is Kapruka's **Authority/Mechanism** in advertising terms. It is real, it is hard for competitors to match, and it directly answers the deepest fear of the highest-value customer (the overseas gifter who can't be there in person).

> **✅ CONFIRMED PROOF — approved for ad use (2026-07-04).** These are the stats copy may state as fact; anything sharper still needs sign-off.
> - **23+ years** in operation.
> - **Island-wide delivery** — anywhere in Sri Lanka.
> - **Same-day delivery within the Colombo region; next-day island-wide.**
> - **Millions of gifts delivered** — phrase as "millions of gifts delivered," not a precise count.
> - **From anywhere in the world → Sri Lanka.** Top diaspora markets: **Australia · USA · Canada · UAE.**

## 2. Business Lines

Kapruka's advertising splits into **three business lines** because the buyer mindset, the emotional drivers, and the proof requirements are completely different. They should **never** share a creative pack.

### Line A — Gifting
The buyer is **not** the consumer of the product. They are sending something to someone else, usually tied to an occasion or relationship. The purchase is **emotionally driven** — it's about the relationship, the moment, and not being seen to fail the recipient. Price sensitivity is lower; *trust that it arrives correctly* is everything.

**Two geographic sub-types of gifting** (this distinction matters more than any demographic):

- **A1 — International → Local.** The sender is overseas (diaspora: Middle East, Australia, UK, Italy, Canada, etc.) sending to family/loved ones in Sri Lanka. This is Kapruka's highest-value, highest-moat segment. The core emotion is **distance, guilt, and presence** — "I can't be there, but the gift can." Trust and reliability fears are at their peak here because the sender cannot verify anything themselves.
- **A2 — Local → Local.** The sender is in Sri Lanka sending to someone elsewhere in Sri Lanka (or arranging delivery they don't want to do in person). Core emotion is **convenience, occasion pressure, and thoughtfulness** — "I want it handled well without me driving across the country."

> **✅ VOLUME SPLIT (confirmed 2026-07-05): gifting is ~70% Local→Local, ~30% Diaspora.** Diaspora is the highest-*value* / highest-moat / highest-intensity segment — but **Local is the majority of the volume.** So **weight gifting idea generation ~70% Local / ~30% Diaspora.** Don't over-index on the diaspora story just because it's the most emotional; most Kapruka gifting is one Sri Lankan sending to another (convenience, occasion pressure, "handle it for me without the drive").

### Line B — Corporate (B2B / bulk / branded gifting) — Local only
The buyer is a **company gifting at scale, within Sri Lanka** — to its own **staff/employees** or to its **customers/clients** — often **many gifts at once**, branded or customised. The driver is **professional obligation and reputation management**, not personal warmth. Distinct economics: bulk pricing, customisation/branding, invoicing, and dated reliable delivery *at scale*. This is its own business with its own buyer, its own proof needs, and its own packs — **never folded into Gifting.**

**Two personas (Local only):**
- **Staff / employees** — the company gifts its own workforce (festive hampers, appreciation, onboarding, milestones).
- **Customers / clients** — the company gifts the people it serves (retention, festive, year-end, deal-close).

**Bulk · branded · customised · invoiced · at-scale** is the line's *characteristic* (how it's delivered), applied to either persona — not a separate persona. *(An individual sending a boss/colleague/in-law a personal-professional gift is **Gifting · Social/Obligation**, not Corporate.)*

> Competitor signal: FNP runs a dedicated advertised "Corporate" lane. Confirmed by the business as a key line (2026-06-20).

### Line C — Ecom (Self-Purchase)
The buyer **is** the consumer. They are shopping for themselves — electronics, groceries, household, fashion, books, etc. The purchase is **practically driven** — price, availability, convenience, delivery speed, selection. Emotion plays a smaller role; the ad must win on *value, trust, and ease*.

> **Strategic emphasis — be known as an e-commerce platform, not only gifting.** Kapruka is widely seen as a *gifting* brand; the goal is to also be top-of-mind for everyday self-purchase. So **Ecom is deliberately over-weighted in idea generation — at least as many ideas as Gifting** (Gifting volume stays the same; Corporate stays the same). Ecom has one persona (Self-Purchaser), so the extra volume comes from its **angle facets** (value/deal · convenience · category-specialist) and **category breadth** (electronics, groceries, household, fashion, books, appliances). *(Still no SKU/price/offer specifics until flagged — broad value/trust/selection/ease only.)*

- **Local Self-Purchase only.** Sri Lanka-based shoppers buying for their own use/household.
- *No product/offer advertising (SKUs, prices, deals) until the human flags it — Ecom ads currently run on broad value/trust/selection/ease.*

> **✅ CONFIRMED (2026-07-04): there is NO international self-purchase segment.** International traffic is **gifting only** (world → Sri Lanka). Do not build Ecom/self-purchase ads for a diaspora audience.

## 3. Why the Lines Stay Separate

| | Gifting (A) | Corporate (B) | Ecom (C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who consumes it | Someone else (recipient) | Many recipients (clients/staff) | The buyer |
| Primary driver | Emotional (relationship, occasion) | Professional obligation, reputation | Practical (value, convenience) |
| Deepest fear | "It won't arrive right / I'll look like I don't care" | "Inconsistent/cheap-looking at scale → professional embarrassment" | "I'll overpay / it won't come / wrong item" |
| What wins the ad | Trust, reliability, emotional resonance | Bulk reliability, branding, taste, invoicing | Value, selection, delivery, ease |
| Proof that matters | "It arrived, on the day, looked great" | Volume/bulk pricing, customisation, dated delivery at scale | Ratings, in-stock, fast delivery |
| Seasonality | Very high (occasion-driven) | High (festive/corporate seasons) | Moderate (seasonal) |

Mixing any two in one pack confuses Meta's signal and dilutes the message. New line = new pack, always.

## 4. The Persona Grid (the map — detail lives in the Personas doc)

Personas are **broad relationship situations**, not demographics. They are the *exploration unit* — kept broad so research and idea generation stay wide. The individual ad is where you sharpen (via optional modifiers: occasion, emotion). Full pain/desire/fear breakdowns + the fine-grained **angle menu** live in `02-personas.md`.

**Geography is a primary split for Gifting and Corporate** — International → Local and Local → Local carry different emotions and proof needs, so each gets the full set. Ecom is Local only.

### Line A — Gifting (each persona exists in BOTH geographies)

| # | Persona (relationship situation) | International → Local (diaspora sender) | Local → Local (in-country sender) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Romantic** | partner/spouse split across countries | girlfriend/boyfriend, wife/husband in SL |
| 2 | **Parents** (mother / father / general) | diaspora child → ageing parents back home | local child → parents elsewhere in SL |
| 3 | **Kids** (baby girl / boy / general) | parent abroad → own child raised in SL | parent → child in another part of SL |
| 4 | **Siblings** (brother / sister) | diaspora → sibling back home | local → sibling |
| 5 | **Friends** | diaspora → friend back home | local → friend |
| 6 | **Social / Obligation** (weddings, funerals/almsgiving, new baby, housewarming, **boss/colleague/in-law personal-professional gifts**) | can't attend from abroad — "send something appropriate" | can't/won't deliver in person |

> **6 relationships × 2 geographies = 12 gifting personas.** The diaspora (International) row carries Kapruka's moat and highest emotional intensity; the Local row runs on convenience and occasion-pressure.

### Line B — Corporate (Local only)

| # | Persona (corporate situation) | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Staff / employees** (appreciation, festive hampers, onboarding) | company → its own staff |
| 2 | **Customers / clients** (retention, festive, year-end) | company → the people it serves |

> **2 corporate personas (Local only).** Driver is professional obligation + reputation; proof needs (the line's *characteristic*) are bulk pricing, branding/customisation, invoicing, and dated delivery at scale. *Bulk/branded is a characteristic, not a persona. Boss/colleague/in-law personal gifts → Gifting · Social/Obligation.*

### Line C — Ecom (Self-Purchase)

| # | Persona | Geography |
|---|---|---|
