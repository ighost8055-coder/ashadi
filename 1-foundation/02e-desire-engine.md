# Kapruka — Desire Engine (Desire · Pain · Fear · Misconception · Belief)

> **What this is:** a raw-material bank of the five psychological forces that move Kapruka's customers — **25 Desires · 25 Pains · 25 Fears · 25 Misconceptions · 25 Beliefs**. It is an **angle-generation source**, not a persona doc. The Researcher, Ideator, and Strategist mine it to produce ad angles *in addition to* (and sometimes replacing) the competitor-library angles.
>
> **Source method:** built on the **Nick Theriot "Human Desire" primer** (`Scale-FB-Ads-Using-Human-Desire-Primer.html`). Core idea: *an ad sells a **desire** — the far side of a bridge the customer wants to cross — and the product is the car that carries them across.* You don't sell dated island-wide delivery; you sell *"she opens the door on her birthday and you're 8,000 miles away but you're still the one who showed up."* Delivery is the bridge.
>
> **Honesty lock:** every angle drawn from this engine still obeys Core Rule 9 + the Three-Yes Test, and leans on the **confirmed moat/proof stats** (23+ yrs · island-wide · same-day Colombo, next-day island · millions delivered · AU/USA/CA/UAE). Misconceptions are busted with *truth*, never spin.

## HOW THE RESEARCHER USES THIS (the two new moves)

Alongside its usual competitor-library mining, the Researcher now does two extra things and **tags every angle in the report with its source**:

**1. Ladder the product (the iPod breakdown).** For each Kapruka offer, climb *Feature → Performance → Benefit → Desired state* — the desire lives on the top rung, and it's usually *shown*, not stated.

> **Worked example — Kapruka dated delivery:**
> | Feature | Performance | Benefit | **Desired state (the ad's real subject)** |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Same-day Colombo / next-day island-wide, 23 yrs | The cake is on her table *on the actual day*, anywhere in Sri Lanka | You never have to gamble on whether it arrives, or apologise for it being late | **"I wasn't there, but I was the one who showed up. She knows I remembered."** |
> | Order from AU/USA/CA/UAE → delivered in SL | You handle it from your phone at 2am your time | The 8,000 miles stop mattering for one moment | **"Distance didn't win today. I was present."** |

**2. Mine this engine for angles.** Pull a Desire (or a Pain/Fear/Misconception/Belief), pair it with a persona + the moat, and write the angle. Rank by **size** (how many customers feel it) × **intensity** (must-be-nice → bleeding-neck) × **season** (where it sits on the occasion calendar right now).

### The source tags every angle must carry in the report
- **`[Ad-Library]`** — mined from a competitor's proven ad.
- **`[Desire-Engine]`** — built from an item in this file (name which: e.g. `D3`, `F1`, `M9`).
- **`[Ladder]`** — surfaced by the Feature→Desired-state deconstruction above.
- **`[Blend]`** — **two or more sources combined** (e.g. `Ad-Library × D4`, or `M1 × B9`). Blends are encouraged — they're where the freshest, least-copyable angles come from. Always list the ingredients.

## INTENSITY & SEASON (score every angle before it's ranked)

- **Intensity ladder** (from the primer): `must-be-nice → annoying → keeps-me-up-at-night → bleeding-neck`. The hotter the desire, the less price matters and the faster they buy. **Kapruka's bleeding-neck windows are the shipping-cutoff days before a Tier-1/2 occasion** (Valentine's, Mother's Day, Christmas, Avurudu) — the man who hasn't sent anything yet, cutoff looming, buys the first credible option at any price.
- **Season** ties to `03-occasion-calendar.md` — the same desire breathes through the year. Mother's-Day guilt is bleeding-neck in early May and a must-be-nice in September. Score *"is it hot right now?"*, not just *"is it a strong desire?"*

> **Segment tags** on each item below: `[Dia]` diaspora Gifting Int→Local · `[Loc]` local Gifting · `[Corp]` Corporate · `[Ecom]` self-purchase · `[All]`. Weighted toward `[Dia]` — the highest-value, highest-intensity segment.

## USING THE ENGINE — quick method for the Researcher / Ideator / Strategist

1. **Pick the segment + occasion** you're generating for (from the priority list + calendar).
2. **Pull the hottest items** across the five lists for that segment — favour high **size × intensity × season**. A bleeding-neck Fear or a Tier-1-season Desire outranks a must-be-nice one.
3. **Build the angle** = *[Desire/Pain/Fear/Misconception/Belief] + persona + the moat as the bridge*. Name the desired state; let dated island-wide delivery be the car across.
4. **Blend for freshness** — combine two items (e.g. `F17 × B15`: "sending cash to avoid caring" fear × "real beats cash" belief → *"Money in her account isn't the same as a cake on her table"*). Blends are the least-copyable angles; always tag the ingredients.
5. **Tag the source** in the report: `[Desire-Engine: D#/P#/F#/M#/B#]`, `[Ladder]`, `[Ad-Library]`, or `[Blend: X × Y]`.
6. **Honesty gate** — run every line through the Three-Yes Test; bust misconceptions only with confirmed facts; never fake a stat.
