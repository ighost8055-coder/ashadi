# Kapruka — General Brand Guidelines (readable version)

## 1. Brand Essence
Premium online gifting — trusted, warm, joyful, polished. Make everyday ecommerce feel like a high-end gifting experience.
- Premium gifting, **not** cheap retail.
- Warm emotion, **not** cold corporate.
- Clean confidence, **not** marketplace clutter.
- Sri Lankan relevance with global-quality presentation.

**Keywords:** Premium · Giftable · Warm · Trustworthy · Celebratory · Fast · Elegant · Friendly · Modern · Scroll-stopping

## 2. Color System
Purple is the Kapruka world. Yellow/gold is the smile, highlight, CTA, premium glow.

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Purple | `#241044` | depth backgrounds (very soft, secondary only) |
| **Retail Purple** | `#340A5C` | **main background (dominant)** |
| Kapruka Yellow | `#FFC107` | highlights / CTA |
| Luxury Gold | `#D9A441` | trims / ribbons |
| Warm Gold | `#F6C96A` | glow / lighting |
| Soft Cream | `#F5F4EB` | light backgrounds (product grids) |

**Ratio:** 70% purple · 15% white/neutral · 10% yellow/gold · 5% category accent (flowers, cakes, seasonal).

## 3. Typography
Rounded, bold, modern sans-serif. Big, friendly, premium, mobile-readable.
- **Headline:** Poppins ExtraBold / Bold, **white**.
- **Subtext:** Poppins SemiBold/Medium or DM Sans Medium, **yellow or soft white**.
- **Body:** DM Sans / Poppins Regular.
- 1080×1350 sizes: hero headline 92–125px, two-line 80–105px (line-height 0.88–0.98), subhead 34–44px, CTA 34–46px, logo width 190–230px top center.

## 4 & 10. Layout — minimal-first, strict
- **One clear idea**, understood in 2 seconds.
- Only the hierarchy the message needs: logo, headline, one optional subline/CTA.
- Safe margins ≥ 70–90px. Main text within ~50–60% of canvas width.
- **White space is mandatory and intentional.** Do not fill empty areas with props, icons, gradients, patterns, cutouts.
- **Do not enlarge** type/logo/product/decoration just to fill canvas. Scale is intentional, not maximized. Before approval, try a 10% smaller version and keep it if calmer.
- Highlight **no more than one** phrase/word in yellow.
- Logo top-center (social), white wordmark + yellow smile on purple; clear space ≥ smile height. Never stretch/tilt/recolor/redraw the smile.

## 6. Imagery & Art Direction
- **Imagery is optional.** When the message works on type alone, stay text-led and uncluttered.
- Background: flat **Retail Purple `#340A5C`** (or very close). Deep Purple only for near-invisible depth. **Avoid obvious gradients, heavy shadows, strong lighting.**
- Prefer **real product photography or clean flat graphics**. Make cakes appetizing, flowers fresh, gifts beautifully packed, hampers abundant but organized.
- **Do NOT** use glossy 3D renders, fake product scenes, artificial mobile screens, plastic-looking visuals — unless explicitly requested for a concept.
- One hero product (large/clear) only when product-focused. Curated clusters for category/seasonal.

## 7. Copy Voice
Simple, confident, slightly playful, instantly understood. Social-first, not brochure.
- **Formula:** Big thought + emotional hook + one clear action.
- Tone examples — Premium: "Beautifully packed. Ready to delight." · Playful: "Warning: cravings start here." · Emotional: "For the people you miss most." · Functional: "Same-day delivery available."

## 8. DO / DON'T
**DO:** one strong idea · highlight only the most important word in yellow · large mobile-readable type · premium lighting + clean product focus · carousel consistency · start simple · protect breathing room · background near `#340A5C` with very soft depth.
**DON'T:** overfill with products · many font styles · let yellow dominate · random gradients/noisy textures · generic-template look · filler details / artificial 3D · enlarge to fill canvas · heavy shadows/strong depth.

## 9. Sizes
- IG portrait carousel / **Meta ad feed: 1080×1350** (premium storytelling) — default.
- IG square / offer clarity: 1080×1080.
- Story/reel cover: 1080×1920 (text in center safe zone).

## 11. Production & Export Standard (critical for the pipeline)
- **AI-generated visuals are CONCEPT REFERENCES / backgrounds only.** Do **not** rely on AI for final logo, typography, or detailed icons.
- Rebuild final logo, text, and line icons as **editable vector layers** (Figma/Canva/Illustrator).
- Use a solid vector rectangle filled `#340A5C` as the final background layer.
- Export from the editable master; check at **800% zoom** — text edges, icon lines, logo, background must stay clean. Confirm spelling + undistorted logo.

## 12. Negative / Avoid List (for image generation)
No distorted logo · no unreadable text · no spelling mistakes · no crowded composition · no cheap supermarket feel · no overused stock-photo look · no random background colors · no excessive shine / plastic look · no awkward hands or faces · no glossy 3D gift boxes / fake cakes / artificial scenes / fake app screens (unless requested) · no unnecessary props/ribbons/icons/stars/fillers · no oversized type/logo/product/CTA to fill space · no obvious gradients/heavy shadows/strong depth — keep background close to `#340A5C`.

## 13. QA Checklist
Idea clear in 2s? · logo clean + correctly placed? · headline mobile-readable? · one idea only? · yellow as highlight not clutter? · product premium/desirable? · safe margins? · feels Kapruka not generic? · spelling correct? · clear CTA? · could any element be removed? · intentionally simple not over-designed? · imagery natural/necessary not artificial? · breathing room preserved? · would slightly smaller feel more premium? · background near `#340A5C` with soft depth? · has every element earned its place?
