# Kapruka — Occasion Calendar

> **Purpose:** This is the calendar layer for **occasion-led generation**. When you generate ideas for a period (a month at a time), the generator reads this file, pulls every occasion landing inside the window **+ its lead time**, and seeds occasion-led ideas alongside the evergreen persona ideas. Occasions don't replace personas — they *activate* them.
>
> **Receiver-country rule:** The recipient is in **Sri Lanka**, and the gift is celebrated on the **Sri Lankan date** — regardless of where the sender lives. So every variable occasion (Mother's Day, Father's Day, etc.) uses the **Sri Lankan date**, even for diaspora senders. One date per occasion.
>
> **Scope:** Major religious festivals across **all faiths** (Buddhist, Christian, Hindu, Muslim) + international gifting/persona "days." Sri Lanka spans every faith and the diaspora is global, so the calendar is broad, not single-religion. *Minor monthly Poya days are intentionally excluded* — only the major festival (Vesak) is kept.
>
> **Status:** Dates below are **confirmed for 2026** (sourced from the official Sri Lanka 2026 holiday calendar). Lunar occasions (Eid, Avurudu, Vesak, Deepavali, Holi, Pongal) shift every year — **re-confirm dates annually** before building the next year's calendar.

---

## THE LEAD-TIME RULE (why this calendar looks ahead, not at "this month")

Gifting ads can't run *on* the occasion. The buyer must see the ad, order, and Kapruka must **deliver by the date** — and planners start weeks early. So when you generate for a period, you are building for occasions that land **2–4 weeks *after* you start**, not the ones happening today.

- **Standard occasions:** start ads **~2 weeks** before the date.
- **Major peaks** (Avurudu, Christmas, Valentine's, Mother's Day): start ads **~3–4 weeks** before — demand builds early and diaspora planners commit far ahead.
- **Last-second layer:** a short urgency burst in the final **2–3 days** before the date catches the last-minute scramblers — runs *in addition to* the lead-time campaign, not instead of it.

> **Worked example:** generating in **late June** → you are building for **mid-July through early August** occasions (e.g. Friendship Day, Aug 2), *not* June. The June occasions already needed their ads weeks ago.

## REVENUE PRIORITY — where to concentrate the idea budget

> **✅ Confirmed by the owner (2026-07-04).** This is the *revenue* ranking, and it is **not** the same as breadth-of-personas — Valentine's and Mother's Day activate narrow personas (Romantic; mother) yet out-earn the broad peaks. Weight the monthly idea budget toward the higher tiers.

- **Tier 1 — top revenue:** **Valentine's Day · Mother's Day.** The two biggest gifting-revenue occasions of the year. Over-invest here — more ideas, earlier lead time, the sharpest swings.
- **Tier 2 — major:** **Christmas · Sinhala/Tamil New Year (Avurudu).** Broad, all-persona peaks; large volume even if below Tier 1 on revenue.
- **Tier 3 — notable:** **Halloween · Easter**, plus the **always-on personal occasions** (birthdays, anniversaries) which collectively carry significant year-round revenue.

## HOW TO READ THIS WITH THE PERSONA GRID

An occasion is a **trigger**, not a persona. It activates one or more personas, and the ad is still built persona-first:

1. Pull the occasion(s) inside the generation window (+ lead time) from the table above.
2. For each, take the **personas it activates** and build occasion-led ideas for them.
3. The occasion is the *hook*; the persona's emotion is the *message*.

> **Example:** Mother's Day (May 10) activates **Parents — International → Local**. The hook is Mother's Day; the message is the diaspora child's distance-guilt ("I can't be there to hand her flowers"). Same occasion, **Local** Parents persona → different message (convenience, not distance).

## ALWAYS-ON (NOT on this calendar — personal triggers)

These fire **year-round, per person**, not on a fixed date, so they live on the persona, not here. Generate them as evergreen ideas every month regardless of the calendar:

- Birthdays · Anniversaries · Weddings · New baby · Housewarming · Funerals / sympathy
- Congratulations / achievement (new job, graduation, exam results, promotion)
- Get-well · Sorry / apology · Thank-you · "Just because" / general reminder
