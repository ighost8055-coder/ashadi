# Kelly's Content Prompt Plays (Kapruka Organic Content — toolkit)

> A library of reusable **content "plays"** Kelly (and the human) pull from on demand. These are *not* the monthly run — that's `09-content-strategist-kelly.md` and the `kelly` agent (`.claude/agents/kelly.md`). These are sharp, single-purpose prompts for: finding fresh ideas, reverse-engineering what works, and pressure-testing an idea or post before it ships.
>
> **How to use:** pick a play, paste in the post/idea/URL it needs, run it. Each play names its input and its output.
>
> **Two standing rules for every play (non-negotiable, from Kelly's role):**
> 1. **Honest claims only.** Never invent a stat, a testimonial, or a delivery promise Kapruka can't truthfully keep. Respect every `[CONFIRM]` flag. Kapruka's trust is the asset — virality is never worth spending it. A "shareable" line that overstates is a fail, not a win.
> 2. **On brand, on the moat.** The niche is Kapruka's world — gifting (Int + Local diaspora), corporate gifting, and e-commerce — anchored on the delivery moat (reliable, dated, island-wide delivery from anywhere in the world to anywhere in Sri Lanka, 20+ years). Keep the three lines separate; never blend them in one idea.

---

## PLAY A — Find content ideas that haven't been done *(idea generation)*

**Use when:** the idea well feels stale, or you want a fresh injection outside the usual pillars.
**Input:** the niche/line to target (default: all of Kapruka; or narrow to one line/persona).
**Output:** 50 original ideas, grouped and ranked by save potential.

```
Act as a content strategist for Kapruka. The niche is [NICHE — default: online gifting + e-commerce
delivery to/within Sri Lanka, incl. the diaspora gifting-home angle]. Generate 50 original content
ideas that are DIFFERENT from what everyone else in this niche is posting (avoid generic "top 10 gifts"
listicles and obvious festival posts).

Group them into:
- Contrarian opinions
- Mistakes (people make when gifting / shopping / sending home)
- Myths (about online delivery, gifting, shopping in SL)
- Case studies (a real send, a real save, a real corporate order — mark any as [CONFIRM] if it needs a true example)
- Frameworks (a repeatable way to choose / plan / send)
- Industry predictions

Rank them from most to least likely to generate SAVES.
Every idea must respect Kapruka's honesty rule (no invented stats/claims) and lean on the delivery moat
where relevant. Keep the three lines (Gifting · Corporate · Ecom) separate — never blend them in one idea.
```

---

## PLAY B — Reverse-engineer a viral post *(analysis → new ideas)*

**Use when:** you find a post (competitor, adjacent niche, or your own top performer) that popped and want to learn *why* and clone the mechanism, not the post.
**Input:** the post (paste text, screenshot, or URL).
**Output:** a teardown + 10 original Kapruka ideas built on the same triggers.

```
Analyze this viral post: [PASTE POST / URL / SCREENSHOT].

Break down:
- Why people STOPPED scrolling (the hook mechanism — first line / first frame / visual)
- The psychological triggers used (curiosity gap, status, identity, fear-of-missing, social proof, novelty…)
- Why people SAVED it (utility / reference value)
- Why people SHARED it (what it says about the sharer)

Then create 10 completely ORIGINAL post ideas for Kapruka using the same principles — mapped to our
lines/personas (Gifting Int/Local · Corporate · Ecom) and the delivery moat. Borrow the mechanism, not
the words or the subject. Honesty rule applies: no borrowed claim we can't truthfully make.
```

---

## PLAY C — Make a post more shareable *(rewrite + score)*

**Use when:** an idea is decided but the draft feels flat before it ships.
**Input:** the post/caption draft.
**Output:** a stronger rewrite + a 1–10 score on both versions so you can see the lift.

```
Review this post: [PASTE POST].

Rewrite it to increase:
- Curiosity
- Specificity
- Emotion
- Storytelling
- Shareability

Keep it TRUE — no invented stats, no delivery promise Kapruka can't keep; specificity must be real
specificity, not fake precision. Stay in Kapruka's voice and on the delivery moat where it fits.

Then score BOTH versions 1–10 on each of the five dimensions above, and say in one line what the rewrite
changed and why it lifts the score.
```

---

## PLAY D — Predict which idea will go viral *(score + fix the weak ones)*

**Use when:** you have a list of candidate ideas (e.g. a fresh Idea Bank, or Play A's output) and want to rank/triage before committing production time.
**Input:** the list of content ideas.
**Output:** a scored table; every idea under 8/10 rewritten.

```
Score these content ideas 1–10 on each of:
- Scroll-stopping potential
- Save potential
- Shareability
- Originality
- Curiosity
- Business value (does it move Kapruka's gifting / corporate / e-commerce goals, or just chase reach?)

[PASTE IDEAS]

Show a table (idea × the six scores + an average). Then REWRITE every idea scoring below 8/10 average so
it clears 8 — sharper hook, tighter specificity, or a stronger tie to the delivery moat — without breaking
the honesty rule. Flag any idea that scores high on reach but low on business value as "reach-only" so we
don't over-invest in it.
```

---

## Notes

- **Business value guards against empty virality.** Plays C and D both weight (or check) business value on purpose — a post that goes viral but doesn't build Kapruka's authority or move a line is a vanity win. The moat and the three lines are the tie-back.
- **Feeds the flywheel.** A standout idea surfaced by Play A/B, or a high scorer from Play D, is a candidate to flag to the Strategist as a paid angle (Kelly's one bridge to the paid track) — same as any strong organic idea.
- **These are Kelly's tools, but line-agnostic.** Any of the four also works for a single line or persona — just narrow the input.
